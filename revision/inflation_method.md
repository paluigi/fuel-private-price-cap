# Inflation method (Step 09 deliverable)

Reference days 1/11/21 Sep and Oct (ISTAT FAQ convention). Observed series:
effective-day national means (universe) with equal station weights.
No-cap series: baseline ARDL counterfactual gross (tax calendar applied
identically). NIC weight: ECOICOP 07.2.2 'Carburanti e lubrificanti' =
4.5219% (45,219 per 10,000, read from istat_ponderazioni_2026.xlsx; the
class includes lubricants, a small residual documented as such).

Fuel split: 50/50 petrol/diesel baseline (author premise: approximately
equal contributions); sensitivities 45/55 and 55/45.
Station selection: universe (equal weights) baseline; ISTAT-like
regularity-filtered variant (>= 40 distinct pre-cap communications in
Jul 1-Sep 27; 57.5% of units; premise-based, labelled as such — no claim
of replicating the proprietary sample).
Preliminary-vs-final releases: author institutional premise that this
component is complete at the preliminary release; not verifiable here and
stated as premise.

Baseline result: petrol -2.34% actual vs +5.34% no-cap MoM; diesel +1.58%
vs +10.86%; estimated contribution to October headline NIC inflation:
**-0.38 pp** (petrol -0.17, diesel -0.21), range -0.38..-0.45 across
admissible models, -0.42 under the regularity sample.

Oct-1 measurement note: the 8am snapshot overstates the day-1 effective
mean by 4.2 (petrol) / 4.9 (diesel) c/l because the re-pricing wave
communicated on 30 Sep-1 Oct is only partially captured; this is a
correction to snapshot-based measurement of OUR reconstructed estimator,
not an attribution of error to ISTAT's published index.
